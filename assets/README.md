# Library room background

`listening-room.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on October 20, 2026 for this project. It depicts an empty room and tabletop, not an album collection. The interactive cases, shadows, text, and cover images are rendered separately.

Generation direction: photorealistic empty comfortable room, low tabletop-level frontal camera, realistic matte wood across the lower 35%, softly blurred warm taupe/beige wall, defocused houseplant at the far left, natural daylight from the left, muted warm grading and shallow depth of field. Keep the center empty. No CDs, text, logos, or interface elements.

The PNG is served locally (about 1.6 MB). Album art continues to come from Cover Art Archive. The background is photographic; the cases and their contact shadows are interactive Three.js geometry. This is a layered scene, not a fully modeled room.
